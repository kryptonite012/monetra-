# Monetra user manual

Monetra brings all of your money into one place: bank accounts, cash, cards,
UPI, income, spending, budgets, loans, investments, goals, bills and net
worth. Everything is based on the records you enter. Monetra is **not**
connected to your bank and will never ask for your bank password, UPI PIN,
CVV or OTPs.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Create an account.** Enter your name, email and a strong password (10+
   characters with three of: lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols). A mobile
   number is optional.
2. **Onboarding** takes three short steps:
   - *About you*: occupation, city and approximate monthly income. This only
     personalises insights.
   - *Your accounts*: add your bank accounts, cash in hand, wallets and credit
     cards with today's balance. For a credit card, enter what you owe as a
     negative number (for example `-12000`).
   - *Preferences*: light/dark theme and which alerts to receive by email.
3. **Verify your email** from the banner or from *Security*. Verified contacts
   receive security alerts and password-reset links.

> **Tip:** turn on two-factor authentication in *Security* straight away.

## 2. Dashboard

Your financial command centre:

- **Available balance**: bank, cash and wallet balances combined, plus card
  dues.
- **Income, expenses and savings rate** for this month, quarter or year (use
  the tabs).
- **Cash flow chart**: income and spending bars with a net-savings line.
- **Financial health score (0–100)**: six explained components (savings
  rate, emergency fund, debt-to-income, budget adherence, net worth, credit
  utilisation). Hover a bar to see how it is scored. *Focus* tells you where
  you would gain most.
- **Spending by category**, **net worth**, **upcoming payments** (bills and
  EMIs), **recent transactions** and **savings goals**.

## 3. Accounts

- **Add account** for each savings, salary or current account, cash, wallet
  or credit card. Only enter the *last 4 digits* of account numbers.
- Open an account to see its balance, a six-month in/out chart and a
  **statement** with a running balance (downloadable as PDF).
- **Reconcile**: enter the closing balance from your bank statement. Monetra
  compares it with its own figure. If they differ, you can post an
  *adjustment*, which is recorded openly and never hidden.
- **Close** an account you no longer use. New entries are blocked, and your
  history is kept.
- **Payment methods** (second tab): add UPI IDs (for example
  `name@okhdfcbank`) and cards (network and last 4 digits only) so you can tag
  how you paid.

## 4. Transactions

The transactions page is your complete ledger. Search by text, account, type
or date. The totals for the current filter appear under the filter bar.

- **New entry**: record an expense, income, refund, bank fee, interest or
  adjustment. Leave the category empty and Monetra picks one from the
  description or merchant, and tells you why (for example *Recognised
  "swiggy"*).
- **Transfer**: move money between your accounts. Transferring to a credit
  card account records a card bill payment.
- **UPI payment**: record money you paid or received over UPI. Paste the
  text of a merchant QR code (`upi://pay?...`) to fill in the payee, amount
  and a category hint. Monetra only records UPI payments; it never makes them.
- Click any transaction to change its category or notes, **correct** the
  amount or date, or **reverse** it. Ledger entries are never edited or
  deleted silently. A correction reverses the original and posts the fixed
  entry, so your history stays complete.

## 5. Income

- **Record income** with the gross amount, TDS and other deductions (for
  example PF). The net amount is credited to the account.
- Sources include salary, bonus, business, freelance, rental, interest,
  dividends, capital gains, pension and more.
- See the current financial year's totals, a monthly chart and a breakdown
  by source.
- **Recurring income**: schedule a salary or rent you receive. Monetra can
  record it automatically on each due date, or just remind you.

## 6. Expenses

- **Add expense** with the amount, date, description, merchant, account and
  payment method. Optionally record GST, mark it tax-deductible (80C, 80D,
  HRA, …) or reimbursable.
- **Analytics**: daily, weekly or monthly trend; categories; top merchants;
  spending by payment mode; and a comparison with the previous period.
- Open an expense to **attach receipts** (JPEG, PNG, WEBP or PDF up to 5 MB)
  or **split** it with friends. Monetra tracks who owes you, and *Settle*
  records the money when they pay you back.
- **Recurring expenses**: schedule rent, SIPs or memberships.

## 7. Budgets

- Use the arrows to pick a month, then **create a budget** with limits for
  the categories you want to watch. A parent category such as *Food & Dining*
  includes all of its sub-categories.
- Each limit shows how much you have spent, what remains, a daily allowance
  and a **month-end projection** based on your current pace (an estimate).
- When a category crosses its alert threshold (80% by default) or goes over,
  you get a notification.
- **Copy last month** reuses your limits. The performance chart compares
  limits with actual spending over time.

## 8. Savings goals

- Create goals such as an emergency fund, a vehicle, a house, a holiday,
  education or retirement. Set a target amount, an optional target date and
  a monthly saving plan.
- Monetra shows progress, an **estimated completion date** and, if you have
  a deadline, the monthly amount needed to meet it, plus an *On track* or
  *Behind* badge.
- **Add money** records a contribution. Choose a *from* and *to* account to
  move real money, for example from your salary account to your savings
  account. Use a negative amount to withdraw.
- You are notified at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.

## 9. Loans & EMIs

- **Add loan**: amount, interest rate, tenure and start date. Monetra
  calculates the EMI and the full amortisation schedule. For an older loan,
  enter how many EMIs you have already paid.
- Open a loan to see the schedule (principal and interest for every EMI),
  **mark the next EMI paid**, make a **part-prepayment** (choose a shorter
  tenure or a lower EMI and see the interest saved), or **foreclose** it.
- The summary cards show total outstanding (loans, card dues and other
  liabilities), monthly EMIs and the interest still to pay.
- The **EMI calculator** gives quick what-if quotes.

## 10. Investments

- Add stocks, mutual funds, ETFs, fixed or recurring deposits, gold or SGBs,
  bonds, PPF, EPF, NPS and more, with the first purchase.
- Record buys, sells (average-cost method for realised gains), dividends,
  interest, bonus units, splits and charges. Choosing a cash account posts
  the money movement to your ledger.
- Every value is labelled with its **price source**:
  - *Manually entered*: a price you typed.
  - *Delayed*: official end-of-day mutual fund NAVs from AMFI, when your
    administrator has enabled them.
  - *Calculated*: deposits valued from their interest rate.
  - *No price yet*: shown at cost.
- The portfolio view shows invested value, current value, unrealised and
  realised gains, **XIRR** and asset allocation.

## 11. Assets & liabilities

- Record property, vehicles, gold, jewellery and other assets, and
  **revalue** them over time.
- Record informal liabilities such as money borrowed from family, taxes due
  or deposits, and log repayments.
- **Net worth** = everything you own (cash and bank, investments, assets)
  minus everything you owe (cards, loans, other liabilities). The trend chart
  uses monthly snapshots.

## 12. Bills & subscriptions

- Add bills (electricity, water, gas, internet, mobile, DTH, rent,
  insurance, maintenance, school fees, card bills, taxes) with their due date
  and how often they repeat.
- **Pay** records the payment as an expense and moves the due date forward.
  A card bill payment becomes a transfer to the card account.
- Add subscriptions (OTT, music, cloud, gym). Click **Used today** when you
  use one. Subscriptions with no recorded use for 60+ days are flagged as
  possible savings.
- You are reminded before due dates (3 days by default) and alerted when a
  bill is overdue.

## 13. AI financial assistant

Ask questions in plain English, for example:

- *Where did my money go this month?*
- *How much did I save this year?*
- *Which category consumes most of my income?*
- *How much EMI do I have remaining?*
- *Can I afford a purchase of ₹50,000?* (amounts like "1.5 lakh" work too)
- *What will my savings look like after 6 months?*
- *How much did I spend on food last month?*

Every answer shows the **facts** it used, its **assumptions and method**, a
**confidence** level and the period it covers. Answers come only from your
own records. Forecasts show a likely range and are estimates, not
guarantees. The **Insights** panel highlights spending changes, unusual
expenses, recurring charges you have not tracked, unused subscriptions and
budget risks.

## 14. Reports

Choose a report, a period and a format (PDF, Excel or CSV):

- Monthly financial statement
- Annual report
- Income vs expenditure
- Account statement
- Tax summary (gross income, TDS, deductions by section, realised capital
  gains, GST paid)
- Loan statement
- Investment report
- Net worth statement

**Preview** shows the report on screen. **Generate** creates the file and
keeps it in your report history for download later. Reports are not audited
statements or tax advice.

## 15. Notifications

You receive alerts for unusual spending, budget thresholds, upcoming EMIs and
bills, subscription renewals, goal milestones and security events. Choose
which appear in the app and which are emailed in *Profile → Notifications*.
Security alerts always appear.

## 16. Security

- **Two-factor authentication**: scan the QR code with an authenticator app,
  confirm a code, and save your recovery codes somewhere safe.
- **Password**: changing it signs out your other devices.
- **Contact verification** for email and mobile.
- **Active sessions**: see every signed-in device and revoke any of them, or
  sign out all others.
- **Sign-in activity** and your personal **audit trail** of account changes.

## 17. Profile & your data

- Update your personal details, financial year start month, time zone and
  theme.
- **Download my data** exports everything as JSON.
- **Delete my account** permanently erases your account, ledger, receipts and
  reports. You need your password, the phrase `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` and, if
  enabled, a 2FA code.

## FAQ

**Why doesn't my balance match my bank?** Monetra only knows what you record.
Use *Reconcile* on the account to find the difference and, if needed, post an
adjustment.

**Can I delete a transaction?** You can *reverse* it. The entry and its
reversal stay in your history, so your records remain trustworthy.

**Is the health score my credit score?** No. It is an educational indicator
calculated from your own records.

**Where do investment prices come from?** Every holding shows its price
source. Unless official AMFI NAVs are enabled for mutual funds, prices are
the ones you enter.
